# AI/ML problem statement

Language tools often assume one standard language and one spelling system. Natural speech can switch languages within a sentence, use regional words alongside English, and produce uncertain spellings when transcribed by ear. FieldProof focuses on preserving the original speech, exposing word-level uncertainty for human review, and presenting a separate translation without silently replacing what was heard.

The speech studio offers ten spoken-language choices plus Auto with local models. The Language Lab is a narrower, explainable text baseline for English, Hindi and Telugu examples. Its language tags are heuristic and should not be presented as the accuracy of the speech model.
